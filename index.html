How it works

Live rating in three simple steps

From projector to podium with zero app installs.

Step 01

Host starts a session

The coordinator enters presentation titles, gets a 6-character code, and puts the big-screen projector page on display.

Step 02

Audience rates 1 to 5 stars

Audience scans the QR code from their seats. Silent anonymous sign-in — no forms or passwords. Tap 1 to 5 stars per pitch.

Step 03

Live screen & podium

The big screen updates live with average ratings and distribution bars. At the end, the podium crowns the top 3 winners.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your event starts.

Do audience members need to sign up or download an app?

No. When an audience member scans the QR code or enters the code, a silent anonymous session is created in the background. No email, password, or app download is required.

How is duplicate voting prevented?

The database enforces a strict unique constraint on (presentation_id, voter_id). A voter can change their rating while the rating window is open, but they cannot submit multiple votes for the same team.

Can the host hide scores while a team is pitching?

Yes. The host console includes a "Hide Scores on Screen" button. While hidden, the projector displays "Voting in progress..." so the room stays focused on the presentation until the host reveals the numbers.

What happens if campus Wi-Fi drops?

Podium uses Supabase Realtime for instant updates and includes an automatic 2-second polling fallback on the big screen. Even if WebSockets disconnect, the screen continues updating seamlessly.

How do ties work on the leaderboard?

Podium uses standard competition ranking: if two teams tie for 1st place with identical average ratings, both show Rank 1 on the top step, and the next team is placed at Rank 3.
